Survey Instruments

System Usability Scale (SUS) Questionnaire

Participants rated each of the 10 SUS statements on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree) after completing all assigned testing tasks.

SUS Scoring Formula

For odd-numbered questions (positive): subtract 1 from the user's score. For even-numbered questions (negative): subtract the user's score from 5. Sum all adjusted scores and multiply by 2.5 to obtain the final SUS score (0–100 scale).

// 01.04
Open-Ended Questions

Qualitative Feedback Instruments

In addition to the SUS scale, participants answered two open-ended questions to capture richer qualitative data about their experience with the system.

Question 01
Improvements & Suggestions

"What improvements or suggestions can you recommend to enhance the usability of the system?"

This question gathered forward-looking suggestions from users — identifying features, interactions, or design changes that would make the system more effective and inclusive for all user types.

Question 02
Most & Least Liked

"What did you like most and least about your experience using the system?"

This question captured immediate emotional reactions — both positive highlights and pain points — allowing the team to understand what aspects of the interface resonated most strongly with users.

Testing Tasks

Six core tasks assigned to participants

Each participant was assigned one of six structured tasks to perform on the CourtSched prototype, covering all major system functions.

Task 01 — 1–2 min limit

Adjust Accessibility Settings

Participants navigated to the accessibility section of the app and adjusted available settings. 7 respondents. Category: Settings.

Settings
Task 02 — 1–3 min limit

Court Reservation

Participants completed the primary court booking flow — selecting a court, date, time slot, and confirming the reservation. 7 respondents.

Core Feature
Task 03 — 1–2 min limit

View Schedule

Participants located the calendar or schedule view and verified current and upcoming court reservations. 6 respondents.

Viewing
Task 04 — 1–2 min limit

View Request

Participants navigated to the reservation request tracking section to view the status of a submitted booking. 7 respondents.

Viewing
Task 05 — 1–2 min limit

View Notification

Participants located and opened the notification panel to check system alerts. Fastest task completed in about 5 seconds by some. 6 respondents.

Edit Profile
