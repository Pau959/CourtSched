// 01
Project Overview

A prototype built to simplify court scheduling for everyone

The CourtSched System is a prototype designed to streamline the process of reserving courts for sports, events, and community activities — making scheduling faster, fairer, and more accessible.

// 01
The Problem

Court scheduling has traditionally been plagued by manual processes, scheduling conflicts, and lack of transparency — leaving athletes, event organizers, and community members frustrated with slow and biased reservation systems.

Manual Booking Scheduling Conflicts Lack of Transparency Poor Accessibility
// 02
The Solution

CourtSched offers a digital platform where users can reserve courts, check availability through a calendar view, track their requests, and receive notifications — all within a clean, minimal interface designed for ease of use.

Court Reservation Calendar View Request Tracking Notifications Profile Management
// 03
Evaluation Method

We conducted a structured usability evaluation combining Jakob Nielsen's 10 Heuristics and a System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire administered to 40 participants who performed 6 core tasks on the prototype.

Heuristic Evaluation SUS Questionnaire Task-Based Testing Open-Ended Feedback

"From the usability test results, most participants found the system convenient, transparent, and easy to use — especially for faster and more organized reservations."
