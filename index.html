// 01
Project Overview

A prototype built to simplify court scheduling for everyone

CourtSched is designed to streamline the reservation of courts for sports, events, and community activities — making scheduling faster, fairer, and more accessible.

// 01
The Problem

Court scheduling has traditionally been plagued by manual processes, scheduling conflicts, and lack of transparency — leaving athletes, event organizers, and community members frustrated with slow and biased reservation systems.

Manual BookingScheduling ConflictsLack of TransparencyPoor Accessibility
// 02
The Solution

CourtSched offers a digital platform where users can reserve courts, check availability through a calendar view, track their requests, and receive notifications — all within a clean interface designed for ease of use.

Court ReservationCalendar ViewRequest TrackingNotificationsProfile Management
// 03
Evaluation Method

We conducted a structured usability evaluation combining Jakob Nielsen's 10 Heuristics and a System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire administered to 40 participants who performed 6 core tasks on the prototype.

Heuristic EvaluationSUS QuestionnaireTask-Based TestingOpen-Ended Feedback
